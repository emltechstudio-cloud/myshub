Privacy Policy

We collect the bare minimum. We protect it fiercely. Here is exactly what we do.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. Our Privacy Promise

At MyShub, we believe your data belongs to you. We do not sell it. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not track you across the internet. We only collect what we absolutely need to run the platform — and we are transparent about every single thing.

💡 In one sentence: We collect your business info (what you choose to share), your approximate location (for analytics only), and basic usage data. That is it. Nothing more.

2. What Data We Collect

A. Information You Provide (Voluntary)

When you create a hub, you choose what to share:

  • Business name and description
  • Category and location (country, state, city)
  • Contact info (WhatsApp number, email, social links)
  • Catalog link and custom links
  • Profile/logo image (optional)

This information is public — it is what customers see when they discover your business. Do not share anything you do not want the public to see.

B. Location Data (Approximate Only)

When someone visits your hub, we detect their approximate location (country and city) from their IP address. We use this for:

  • Showing you where your visitors come from (analytics)
  • Helping customers find businesses near them (search)

📍 Important: We do NOT collect precise GPS coordinates. We do NOT track movement. We do NOT know your exact address. We only know the general city/region — the same information any website can see from your IP address.

C. IP Addresses

We temporarily log IP addresses for:

  • Security (detecting spam, abuse, or attacks)
  • Analytics (counting unique visitors to your hub)

IP addresses are NOT linked to your personal identity. They are NOT shared with third parties. They are automatically deleted after 30 days.

D. Usage Analytics

We track basic usage metrics:

  • How many times your hub was visited
  • Which links were clicked
  • Which platform referred the visitor (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, etc.)
  • What country/city the visitor is from

This data is shown to you in your dashboard. It helps you understand what is working and what is not.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

We deliberately do NOT collect:

  • ❌ Your browsing history outside MyShub
  • ❌ Your personal messages or conversations
  • ❌ Your contacts or address book
  • ❌ Your device camera or microphone
  • ❌ Precise GPS location
  • ❌ Financial information (we do not process payments — Selar/Paystack/Flutterwave do)
  • ❌ Cookies for advertising or tracking

5. Who Can See Your Data

  • Your public hub info — Visible to anyone who visits your hub or finds you in search
  • Your analytics — Visible only to you in your dashboard
  • Your account details (email, password) — Visible only to you and our secure systems

We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT share it with advertisers. We do NOT give it to other companies for marketing.

6. Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted)
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • We use secure hosting infrastructure
  • We regularly review our security practices

However, no system is 100% secure. If we ever discover a security breach, we will notify affected users immediately.

7. Cookies

MyShub uses minimal cookies:

  • Session cookies: To keep you logged in while you use the platform
  • Preference cookies: To remember your theme (light/dark mode)

We do NOT use cookies for advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics. We do NOT use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any external tracking scripts.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Download everything we have about you from your dashboard
  • Update your data: Edit your hub info anytime
  • Delete your data: Delete your account and all associated data permanently
  • Opt out: Stop using the platform at any time

To delete your account, go to your dashboard settings. Deletion is permanent and immediate.

9. Children's Privacy

MyShub is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the platform evolves. If we make significant changes, we will notify you via email or on the platform. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
