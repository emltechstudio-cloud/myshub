How to Sell on WhatsApp Business in Nigeria: The Complete Guide for 2026

📅 August 7, 2026⏱ 8 min read✍ EML Tech Studio

WhatsApp is not just a messaging app in Nigeria — it is the biggest marketplace in the country. With over 90 million active users, more Nigerians buy and sell on WhatsApp than on any e-commerce platform combined. If you are not selling on WhatsApp yet, you are leaving money on the table.

In this guide, we will show you exactly how to set up WhatsApp Business, create a professional catalog, automate your sales process, and turn casual chats into consistent revenue.

💡 Quick stat: 67% of Nigerian online shoppers prefer to buy through WhatsApp over traditional websites.

1. Why WhatsApp is the #1 Sales Channel in Nigeria

WhatsApp dominates Nigerian commerce for three simple reasons:

  • Everyone has it. Unlike Instagram or TikTok, WhatsApp cuts across all demographics.
  • It feels personal. Customers trust businesses they can message directly.
  • No data costs for customers. A simple message costs almost nothing.

2. Setting Up WhatsApp Business (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Download WhatsApp Business

Get it from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. It is completely free.

Step 2: Create Your Business Profile

Add your business name, category, description, address, hours, and email.

Step 3: Set Up Your Catalog

Go to Settings > Business Tools > Catalog and add your products. Each product needs a clear image, name, price, and description.

Step 4: Create Quick Replies

Set up shortcuts for welcome messages, price lists, delivery info, payment details, and thank-you notes.

4. Automating Your WhatsApp Sales

Set up away messages, greeting messages, and labels to organize chats. This keeps you professional even with hundreds of conversations.

5. WhatsApp Marketing Strategies That Work

  • Status Updates: Post product photos and testimonials daily
  • Broadcast Lists: Send targeted messages to different customer segments
  • Link in Bio: Put your MyShub hub link everywhere
  • Referral Program: Ask happy customers to share your number

6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Spamming broadcast lists
  • Ignoring messages (reply within 1 hour)
  • No catalog set up
  • Using personal WhatsApp for business
