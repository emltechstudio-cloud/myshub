MyShub vs Linktree vs Beacons: Which Link-in-Bio Tool is Actually Best for Your Business?

Linktree is the Kleenex of bio link tools — the name everyone knows. But just because something is popular does not mean it is the best choice for your business.

⚡ TL;DR: Linktree is a link page. MyShub is a business network. If you want to get found by customers actively searching for businesses like yours, MyShub wins by a mile.

ToolWhat It IsBest For

2. Discovery: Can Customers Actually Find You?

Linktree: ❌ No discovery. You are invisible unless someone already knows your link.

Beacons: ❌ No discovery. Same problem.

MyShub: ✅ Built-in discovery. Your business appears in search results by category, location, and keywords. Premium shops are featured at the top.

Winner: MyShub — by a landslide.

3. Conversion: Do Visitors Actually Buy?

Linktree: A list of links. High friction, no social proof.

Beacons: Good for creators, basic store for products.

MyShub: Built for sellers — WhatsApp integration, social proof, clear CTAs, professional design.

Winner: MyShub
