How to Grow Your Business by Connecting Your Audience Across Social Media

Every day, millions of potential customers scroll through Instagram, check WhatsApp status updates, watch TikTok videos, and browse Facebook groups. They are looking for products, services, and businesses just like yours. The problem? Your audience is scattered across ten different platforms — and you are sending them ten different links.

What if you could bring them all to one place? One link that connects your WhatsApp catalog, Instagram bio, TikTok profile, Shopify store, and every other platform you use. That is exactly what MyShub does — and in this guide, we will show you how to use this strategy to grow your business faster than ever.

💡 The one-link strategy helped over 1,200 sellers across 80+ countries consolidate their online presence and double their customer reach.

1. The Problem: Your Customers Are Lost in the Link Maze

Here is what the typical seller does:

  • Shares their WhatsApp catalog link on Instagram stories
  • Puts their Selar store link in their TikTok bio
  • Sends their Shopify URL in Facebook DMs
  • Has a Google Form for orders somewhere else

Your customer sees your product on Instagram, wants to buy, but the link in your bio goes to a different page than the one with the product. They get confused. They leave. You lose a sale.

Even worse, if someone discovers you on one platform, they have no easy way to find you on the others. Your Instagram followers do not know you are also on TikTok. Your WhatsApp customers do not know you have a full catalog online. Every platform is an island — and your business is drowning in the ocean between them.

2. The Solution: One Hub, Every Platform

A business hub is a single page that brings together every platform you use. Think of it as your digital storefront — the one place where anyone can find everything about your business.

With MyShub, your hub includes:

  • 🔗 Your catalog link — Selar, Shopify, WooCommerce, or any store URL
  • 📱 Your WhatsApp — Direct chat button for instant orders
  • 📸 Your social profiles — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube
  • 🛒 Your custom links — Google Forms, booking pages, portfolio, anything
  • 📍 Your location — So local customers can find you

Instead of sending five different links, you send one. Instead of hoping customers find the right page, you give them everything in one click.

Why This Works for Nigerian and African Sellers

In Nigeria and across Africa, WhatsApp is the #1 sales channel for small businesses. But WhatsApp alone is not enough. Your customers are also on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. A hub lets you bridge all of them.

When a customer finds you on TikTok, they can instantly jump to your WhatsApp to place an order. When someone sees your product on Instagram, they can browse your full catalog on Selar. Every platform feeds into every other platform.

Most sellers have no idea where their customers come from. They post on Instagram, share on WhatsApp, upload on TikTok — but they cannot tell which platform is actually driving sales.
