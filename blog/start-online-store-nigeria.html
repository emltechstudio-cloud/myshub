How to Start an Online Store in Nigeria Without Coding or Spending a Fortune

📅 August 7, 2026⏱ 8 min read✍ EML Tech Studio

Starting an online store in Nigeria used to mean spending ₦500,000+ on a website or settling for a basic Instagram page. Neither was great. But in 2026, you can launch a professional online presence in under 30 minutes, for free, without writing a single line of code.

🚀 Reality check: You do not need a website to start selling online. You need a hub that connects your catalog, WhatsApp, and social media.

2. What You Actually Need to Sell Online

1. A Product Catalog

  • WhatsApp Business catalog (free)
  • Selar store (free for digital)
  • Google Sheet with photos and prices

2. A Way to Receive Orders

In Nigeria, this is almost always WhatsApp. Customers want to chat, ask questions, and then pay.

3. A Payment Method

  • Bank transfer (most common)
  • Paystack (1.5% fee)
  • Flutterwave (good for international)
  • OPay, PalmPay, Kuda (instant)

4. A Way to Get Discovered

This is where MyShub comes in. Your hub appears in search results when customers search for your category and location.

3. Launch Your Store in 30 Minutes

Step 1: Define Your Niche (5 min)

Pick ONE category: "Handmade jewelry for working women" or "Organic skincare for acne-prone skin."

Step 2: Set Up Your Catalog (10 min)

Download WhatsApp Business, go to Settings > Catalog, and add 5-10 products with clear photos and prices.

Step 3: Create Your Business Hub (5 min)

Create your MyShub hub with your catalog link, WhatsApp button, social profiles, and location.

Step 4: Set Up Payments (5 min)

Share bank details via WhatsApp or create a Paystack account for professional payments.

Step 5: Launch and Share (5 min)

Share your hub link on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, and Twitter.

4. Marketing Without Ads

  • MyShub Discovery: Free organic search visibility
  • Instagram Reels: Short product videos with trending sounds
  • WhatsApp Status: Daily product posts seen by all contacts
  • TikTok: Product demos and packing videos
  • Word-of-Mouth: Deliver exceptional service, ask for referrals

5. Scaling Up

Phase 1 (₦0-₦50k/month): Free MyShub hub + WhatsApp catalog + social media

Phase 2 (₦50k-₦500k/month): MyShub Pro + Selar/Shopify + Paystack + email list

Phase 3 (₦500k+/month): MyShub Premium + custom domain + full e-commerce + team
